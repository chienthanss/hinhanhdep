# License & Usage Terms

Last updated: 2026

Wallpaper Gallery ("we", "us") publishes wallpapers compiled from the internet and made available for **personal, non-commercial use**. This page explains what you may and may not do with the images on this site, and how to request permission for other uses.

## 1. Free for Personal Use

All wallpapers on **hinhanhdep.org** are free to download and use for personal purposes, including:

- Setting as a desktop, laptop, tablet, or phone wallpaper
- Using in personal, non-public projects (e.g. a private slideshow, personal blog header with no monetization)
- Sharing the **link** to the wallpaper page on social media

No attribution is required for personal use, though a link back is always appreciated.

## 2. Not Permitted Without Permission

You may **not**, without prior written permission:

- Use any wallpaper for **commercial purposes** (advertising, products, paid templates, client work, merchandise, etc.)
- Resell, redistribute, or repackage the images as your own
- Upload the images to stock photo sites, print-on-demand services, or AI training datasets
- Remove watermarks or copyright notices
- Claim authorship or copyright over the images

## 3. Commercial Licensing

If you want to use a wallpaper for commercial purposes, contact us first:

**Email:** [license@hinhanhdep.org](mailto:license@hinhanhdep.org)

Please include:

- The wallpaper URL (e.g. `https://hinhanhdep.org/wallpaper/<slug>/`)
- A short description of how you intend to use it
- The expected reach or distribution (optional)

We will respond with licensing terms and any applicable fee. Commercial use without a written license is not permitted.

Wallpapers on this site are **compiled from the internet** and are believed to be in the public domain or shared under permissive terms. We do not claim ownership of the original artwork. If you are a copyright holder and believe an image on this site infringes your rights, please see section 5.

## 5. Copyright Reports / Takedown

If you are a rights holder and want an image removed, email:

**Email:** [report@hinhanhdep.org](mailto:report@hinhanhdep.org)

Please include:

- The wallpaper URL or slug
- Proof of ownership or authorization
- The specific action you are requesting (removal, attribution change, etc.)

We process valid requests promptly and will remove or adjust the content as appropriate.
